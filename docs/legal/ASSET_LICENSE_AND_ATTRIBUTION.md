## Contributor Rule

Do not add third-party assets unless their license allows repository publication and the attribution or license notice is included in the same change.

When adding generated assets, record:

- Source or generation method.
- Date.
- Intended in-project use.
- Any reuse restrictions.

Generated assets with unclear rights should stay out of Git until their status is resolved.
