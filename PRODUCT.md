# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ONOKO ARCANA is for users who want to practice tarot reading on a PC without owning a physical deck. They are in a focused study-and-reading workflow: choose a spread, draw cards, reveal them in order, write their own interpretation, compare against guidance, and revisit saved readings.

## Product Purpose

The product turns generated ONOKO tarot assets into a usable desktop divination table. The immediate MVP medium is a Web/Electron-ready 2D table because it proves the reading loop faster than Unreal wiring. Unreal remains a future premium 3D expression once the product loop is stable. Success means the first screen feels like an active reading table rather than an admin dashboard: cards are clear, the spread state is readable, and the learning loop can be completed from one play surface.

## Brand Personality

Mysterious, precise, and luminous. The interface should feel like a cyber divination instrument: dark acrylic surfaces, blue observational light, restrained gold framing, and calm Japanese study copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid plain gray debug panels, bright dashboard styling, oversized marketing hero layouts, toy-like fantasy decoration, and UI that hides the card table behind controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep the table and cards as the primary experience.
- Use panels as instruments around the table, not as decorative cards.
- Reserve gold for framing, labels, and important reading state.
- Use blue light for selection, reveal, and active guidance.
- Preserve readable Japanese text and familiar controls while the UI becomes more atmospheric.
