# Scope

## In Scope

- Current cross-project ONOKO IP positioning.
- Practical monetization and validation order.
- Role assignment for:
  - ONOKO Command Center
  - ONOKO ARCANA
  - ONOKO ORACLE
  - Yoitomoshi Art Generator
  - ONOKO LoRA/model assets
- First 30-60 day execution direction.
- Guardrails for public claims, licensing, and operational burden.

## Out Of Scope

- Final legal advice.
- Full market sizing with paid data.
- Public release of LoRA/model files.
- Large story bible or complete game design.
- Automated posting to SNS APIs.
- Paid advertising plan.

## Assumptions

- The project is operated primarily by one person.
- Current priority is realistic momentum, not maximum theoretical upside.
- Early public work should be low-cost, reversible, and measurable.
- ONOKO's public identity must remain consistent before expanding to many formats.
