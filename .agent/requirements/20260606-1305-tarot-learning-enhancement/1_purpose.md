# Purpose

## Goal

Strengthen ONOKO ARCANA from a usable tarot reading table into a tarot study application where the user can gradually learn card meanings, spread reading, symbolic interpretation, and their own reading patterns.

## Product Position

ONOKO ARCANA should stay distinct from generic fortune-telling apps. The core experience is not "the app tells the answer"; it is:

1. The user reads first.
2. The app reveals structured guide information after the user's note.
3. The app helps the user revisit, compare, and practice over time.

## Success Criteria

- A beginner can learn Major Arcana meanings without leaving the app.
- The app supports memory: recall, review, repetition, and comparison.
- The app supports interpretation: card meaning, slot meaning, question context, and story synthesis.
- Existing local history is preserved.
- New learning features remain local-first and work in the Web/Electron runtime.

## Non-Goals

- Do not claim prediction accuracy or supernatural certainty.
- Do not expand to Minor Arcana until Major Arcana study flow is stable.
- Do not replace the reading table with a dashboard-first product.
- Do not require accounts, cloud sync, or paid APIs for the core learning loop.
