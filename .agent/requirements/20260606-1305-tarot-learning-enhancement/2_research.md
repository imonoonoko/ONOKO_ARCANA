# Research Notes

## Source Findings

### Tarot History And Structure

- Britannica describes tarot as a card system invented in Italy in the 1430s by adding 21 triumph cards and the Fool to an existing four-suited pack. Product implication: teach tarot as a structured card system, not only as mystical imagery. Source: https://www.britannica.com/topic/tarot
- The V&A notes surviving 15th century Italian hand-painted and gilded tarot cards, and frames early tarot as a trick/trump game for Renaissance court play. Product implication: add an optional history layer and "card as artifact" facts. Source: https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/tarot-cards
- Yale Beinecke's Visconti Tarot material reinforces the importance of historical decks and playing-card collections. Product implication: support a "deck lineage" field later, even if v1 uses ONOKO art. Source: https://beinecke.library.yale.edu/collections/highlights/visconti-tarot
- MIT/French Playing Cards summarizes that Tarot de Marseille included 56 regular cards plus trumps, and that tarot was originally used for games even though it is now widely understood as divination. Product implication: future Minor Arcana schema should include suit, rank, court role, and tradition notes. Source: https://frenchplayingcards.mit.edu/tarot/tarot-deck

### Tarot Learning And Journaling Patterns

- Biddy Tarot's card meanings library exposes upright/reversed meanings, keywords, symbolism, guidance, suggested questions, and context-specific interpretation tips. Product implication: ONOKO card schema should grow from `keywords + studyFocus` into layered study content. Source: https://support.biddytarot.com/hc/en-us/articles/13184136583311-Where-do-I-find-the-Tarot-Card-Meanings-Library
- Labyrinthos positions itself as an online tarot school and highlights keywords, suit correspondences, and numerology basics. Product implication: add bite-size lessons and later suit/numerology modules. Source: https://labyrinthos.co/pages/app
- Tarot Journal focuses on recording spreads, writing thoughts, enriching readings with reflection questions, and tracking patterns such as frequent cards and arcana/suit trends. Product implication: ONOKO's study lens should evolve into review filters, pattern reports, and interpretation comparison. Source: https://tarotjournal.app/

### Learning Science

- A systematic review in Educational Psychology Review found retrieval practice benefits across many classroom settings and formats. Product implication: add recall-first quizzes before showing answers. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-021-09595-9
- A Nature npj Science of Learning review describes the forward testing effect: interim tests during study can improve later learning and retrieval. Product implication: short checks between card lessons are better than only passive reading. Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-018-0024-y
- Research on interleaving retrieval practice indicates mixed practice can support learning beyond blocked study. Product implication: quizzes should mix cards, orientations, and spread positions instead of drilling one card at a time forever. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35436145/

## Product Translation

The strongest learning roadmap is not a single "card encyclopedia" screen. It should combine:

- Structured card knowledge.
- Recall-first practice.
- Guided interpretation after the user's own attempt.
- Review over spaced sessions.
- Pattern detection from saved readings.
- Progressive spread training.
